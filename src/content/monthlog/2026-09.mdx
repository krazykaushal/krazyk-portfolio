## Bought my first domain

So I am now the official owner of the `kaushal.fyi` domain. I have been wanting to buy a domain for a pretty long time since last year when I wanted to host my portfolio on my own domain instead of every other portfolio's first choice with the same `*.vercel.app`.
Currently I have not transferred my portfolio to this domain nor I have deployed in production the converted version of my portfolio from Next.js to Astro.
But I did explored all the self-hosting I could do on Orcale instance and attached subdomanin to each service.

I have bought my domain from Cloudflare as it served the best renewal price comapared to any other provider.
